HORIZON ASTROPHYSICS INSTITUTE

Exploring the Universe through astrophysics and computation.

HASTI is an independent research initiative focused on astrophysics, cosmology, planetary systems, numerical simulations, astronomical data analysis, and machine learning.

ABOUT

Independent research with an international perspective.

HASTI is being established as an independent non-profit research institute dedicated to scientific research and collaboration in astrophysics. Its activities connect theoretical and observational approaches with modern computational methods.

The institute aims to develop research projects, scientific software and collaborations with researchers and institutions in France and abroad.

RESEARCH

Research areas

Planetary Systems

Planet formation, orbital dynamics, long-term stability and N-body simulations.

Galactic hidden gas

Search for missing baryons through scintillation effect.

Computational Astrophysics and AI

Numerical simulations, Machine learning and statistical methods applied to astrophysical data.
