ABOUT

Independent research with an international perspective.

HASTI is being established as an independent non-profit research institute dedicated to scientific research and collaboration in astrophysics. Its activities connect theoretical and observational approaches with modern computational methods.

The institute aims to develop research projects, scientific software and collaborations with researchers and institutions in France and abroad.
